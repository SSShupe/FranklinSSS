Cerebral Cortex — The wrinkled outer layer of the brain, and the seat of higher-order thinking. The folding (gyri = ridges, sulci = grooves) dramatically increases    
  surface area. Divided into two hemispheres connected by the corpus callosum, and subdivided into four lobes.                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                         
  ---
  Frontal Lobe — The largest lobe, at the front. The brain's executive center:                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                         
  - Executive function — planning, decision-making, impulse control, judgment
  - Personality and social behavior — damage here can radically alter who a person is (famously illustrated by Phineas Gage)                                             
  - Motor cortex — the rear strip of the frontal lobe controls voluntary movement                                                                                        
  - Speech production — Broca's area (usually left hemisphere) produces language                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                                                         
  ---                                                                                                                                                                    
  Parietal Lobe — Behind the frontal lobe, at the top:                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                         
  - Somatosensory cortex — processes touch, pain, temperature, and body position
  - Spatial awareness — integrates sensory input to build a sense of where your body and objects are in space                                                            
  - Attention — damage to the right parietal lobe can cause hemispatial neglect, where patients ignore the entire left side of their world                               
                                                                                                                                                                         
  ---                                                                                                                                                                    
  Occipital Lobe — At the very back of the brain. Almost entirely dedicated to vision:                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                         
  - Visual processing — receives raw input from the eyes and processes color, shape, motion, and depth
  - Visual recognition — damage can cause blindness even with perfectly healthy eyes, or strange conditions like being unable to recognize faces (prosopagnosia)         
                                                                                                                                                                         
  ---                                                                                                                                                                    
  Temporal Lobe — On the sides, roughly behind the temples:                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                         
  - Auditory processing — primary hearing cortex lives here
  - Language comprehension — Wernicke's area (usually left hemisphere) understands spoken and written language; damage produces fluent but meaningless speech            
  - Object and face recognition — the "what" pathway for visual identification runs through here                                                                         
  - Memory consolidation — works closely with the hippocampus (which is tucked inside it)
